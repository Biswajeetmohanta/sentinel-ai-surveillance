COMPUTER VISION
Specialized AI ANPR Pipeline for Indian Conditions
Multi-stage pipeline engineered specifically for high accuracy under difficult lighting, angles, and plate formats.
⚡ 5-Step Deep Learning Pipeline
Executes in under 100ms per frame entirely on local hardware
1. Adaptive Sampler: Motion-triggered frame grabbing throttles to 10 FPS to save compute.
2. Dual-Head YOLOv8: Isolates vehicle class (Car, Bike, Truck, Auto) and crops the license plate rectangle.
3. CLAHE Image Enhancement: Normalizes night headlight glare, shadow contrast, and perspective deskew.
4. PaddleOCR (PP-OCRv4): Character recognition trained on Indian multi-font, HSRP, and 2-line plates.
5. Regex Validation: Corrects optical confusions (e.g. 0 vs O, 8 vs B, 1 vs I).
